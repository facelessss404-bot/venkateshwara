PRECISION LABORATORY

Metrology & Testing Equipment

Every production batch undergoes multi-axis dimensional and metallurgical verification.

Optical Profile Projector

Shadowgraph magnification inspection verifies head radius, chamfer lead-in angle, step concentricity, and wall thickness uniformity.

Hardness Testers

Vickers and Rockwell micro-hardness testing monitors metallurgical grain recrystallization before and after annealing furnaces.

Salt Spray Chamber

ASTM B117 salt fog corrosion testing verifies that electroplated zinc and passivated finishes resist white and red rust up to 720+ hours.

Digital Calipers & Gauges

Mitutoyo calibrated micrometers, plug gauges, and concentricity dial indicators ensure adherence to tight tolerances down to ±0.01 mm.
