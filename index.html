MANUFACTURING SUPERIORITY

Why Cold Forging Outperforms Conventional Machining

Unlike turned fasteners that cut across the raw material's crystalline grain structure, cold-heading plasticizes high-tensile wire under extreme pressure, causing continuous metallurgical grain flow to follow the contour of the rivet head and shank.

Enhanced Fatigue & Shear Strength

Interrupted grain lines in turned pins create micro-shear failure points. Cold heading preserves unbroken grain continuity.

Zero Metal Waste & Sustainable Metallurgy

Near 100% material utilization. Metal volume is displaced plastically rather than turned away into scrap chips.

High-Speed Automated Production

Multi-station headers forge up to hundreds of parts per minute, ensuring ultra-competitive pricing and reliable volume delivery.

A SIX-DECADE TRAJECTORY

Our Manufacturing Story Since 1964

1964

Ancillary Inception with TI Cycles of India

Founded by Rm. M.V. Muthiah Chettiar in Karakudi, Chettinad, Sri Venkateswara Company began operations as a dedicated ancillary unit manufacturing bicycle rivets for TI Cycles of India.

STRATEGIC EXPANSION

Industrial Relocation to Coimbatore

Recognizing Coimbatore's emerging stature as the engineering hub of South India, MVM Muthu consolidated operations and established the modern manufacturing facility in Periyanaickenpalayam.

MODERN LEADERSHIP

Over 35 Years Under Jawahar Muthu

Current CEO Jawahar Muthu assumed executive management, bringing over three and a half decades of hands-on expertise in cold-forged rivet engineering and tooling optimization.

JANUARY 2018

Attainment of IATF 16949:2016

Officially secured the globally recognized automotive quality management certification, demonstrating continual defect prevention, variance waste reduction, and rigorous supply chain reliability.

GLOBAL EXPANSION

Export Shipments to Europe & SE Asia

SVC broadened its footprint into international export markets, shipping precision rivets to clients in Germany and Thailand. CONFIRMING CURRENT FREQUENCY
