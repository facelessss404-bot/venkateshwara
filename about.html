ESTABLISHED 1964 • COIMBATORE, TAMIL NADU

Sixty Years of Continuous Forging Travel & Engineering Integrity

From humble beginnings in Karakudi as a precision cycle-rivet supplier for TI Cycles of India, Sri Venkateswara Company has evolved over six decades into a multi-generational precision manufacturing powerhouse serving domestic OEMs and international export destinations.

FOUNDATIONAL HISTORY

The Journey from TI Cycles Ancillary to Global Fastener Specialist

All the way back in 1964, visionary industrialist Rm. M.V. Muthiah Chettiar first founded Sri Venkateswara Company in Karakudi, Chettinad, operating as a specialized ancillary unit to produce heavy-duty rivets for TI Cycles of India.

Subsequently, the venture was managed as a collaborative partnership by his four sons and a relative. With acute foresight regarding South India's industrial manufacturing growth, MVM Muthu acquired the collective partnership shares, relocated the entire manufacturing machinery to Coimbatore, and laid the foundations of the present-day Periyanaickenpalayam plant.

Today, executive management rests under current CEO Jawahar Muthu, who possesses over three and a half decades (35+ years) of deep, hands-on experience in cold-forged rivet engineering, precision tool design, and mass-scale production line orchestration.

International Export Record

Leveraging CEO expertise and disciplined management, SVC expanded into global exports, shipping custom fasteners to engineering clients in Germany and Thailand.
