END-TO-END IN-HOUSE METALLURGY

Integrated Manufacturing & Finishing Services

From precision high-velocity cold forging to controlled atmosphere annealing, 3-6 minute ultrasonic agitation, and an 80-tonne automated electroplating plant. Every critical stage is controlled under one roof in Coimbatore.

CAPABILITY 01

Precision Cold Heading (Cold Forging)

Cold heading is high-speed cold metal forming where coiled wire rod is cut to precise blank lengths and forced into carbide dies under multi-station punches at ambient temperatures.

ZERO CHIP SCRAP

Material is formed plastically rather than cut away, eliminating metal waste.

CONTINUOUS GRAIN

Internal metallurgical crystalline flow lines follow the rivet contours.

METALLURGICAL TRANSFORMATION PIPELINE

The 4-Station Cold Heading Stroke Sequence

CYCLE CADENCE: UP TO 300 PPM
STAGE 01 // CUTOFF

Wire Straightening & Shear

High-tensile wire coil is straightened through carbide rollers and sheared with square cut face into a measured slug.

TOLERANCE: ±0.03 mm LENGTH
STAGE 02 // CONE UPSET

Primary Punch Upset

First punch strikes the protruding wire, gathering metal volume into a symmetrical conical bulb without eccentric grain bending.

PRE-FORM CONE ANGLE: 60°
STAGE 03 // DIE CAVITY

Secondary Finish Header

Finishing punch compresses head into final geometry (round, mushroom, flat) and backward-extrudes tubular tip cavity.

PRESSURE: 80+ TONS/CM²
STAGE 04 // EJECTION

Pneumatic Part Ejection

Positive knockout pin thrusts finished fastener out of the carbide die directly onto sorting conveyer without part collision.

PARTS/MIN: CONTINUOUS FEED
CAPABILITY 02

Annealing (Heat Treatment Normalization)

Severe cold deformation increases the hardness and tensile strain within the rivet metal. Annealing is our specialized thermal process used to normalize the interatomic crystalline space of components following the forging operation.

By heating components to critical recrystallization thresholds and cooling under regulated atmospheres, we significantly increase the ductility and workability of the rivets, ensuring flawless roll-forming and clinching on customer assembly lines without splitting or shank embrittlement.

SPECIFICATION HIGHLIGHT Controlled soak cycle restores elongation percentage up to 35% for effortless automated clinching.
CAPABILITY 03

Ultra-Sonic Agitation Cleaning

Prior to surface plating or packaging, components must be completely free of forging lubricants, micro-chips, and particulate contamination.

Our ultrasonic cleaning process utilizes proprietary chemical solvents in combination with high-frequency sound waves. These ultrasound waves generate millions of microscopic cavitation bubbles that violently implode against the metal surface, thoroughly dislodging debris from deep blind holes and tubular rivet cavities.

DURATION 3 to 6 Minutes Cycle
SOLVENT CAVITATION 100% Blind Hole Penetration
CAPABILITY 04

80-Tonne Automated In-House Plating Plant

Sri Venkateswara Company houses a fully automated, PLC-controlled barrel electroplating facility boasting a heavy industrial throughput capacity of 80 tonnes per month.

By maintaining this monumental finishing capability on-site in Coimbatore, we avoid the quality inconsistency, hydrogen embrittlement risks, and transit delays associated with third-party job platers.
